Two brothers make honest fasting apps. Everything for a story is here.

In one sentence: Pjotr and Kasper Peulen make small apps: fair pricing, no ads, and your data stays on your phone.

Stilvast (expected 5 January 2027): a calm intermittent fasting timer. Durora (expected mid-January 2027): a countdown to iftar with Diyanet times or your own mosque's timetable.

Price: free to download, everything open for 14 days, then pay once per app: Stilvast € 4.99 until 1 April 2027 (then € 6.99), Durora € 4.99.
