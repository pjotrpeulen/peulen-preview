A fasting timer that simply works

  • Start after dinner, stop at your first meal: two taps a day, right from your Lock Screen

  • No streaks, no badges, no missed days

Free to download, everything open for 14 days, then a one-time € 4.99 (until 1 April 2027, then € 6.99). Timing always stays free.

About us

A pea pod has two sides and a few peas lying close together. Our last name is Peulen ("pods" in Dutch), and that's how we work: the two of us, small, close together.

We're Pjotr and Kasper, brothers. Together we come up with and make small apps; Kasper builds them.

We noticed that many simple apps aren't simple anymore: a pricey weekly subscription for a timer, ads between your own data, an account before you can do anything. So we do it differently, and we'll keep it that way.
