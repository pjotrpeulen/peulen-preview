Stilvast: a fasting timer that simply works

Two taps a day. Timing always stays free. Pay once, no ads.

Coming soon to the App Store
Expected: Tuesday 5 January 2027.

What it does

  • Start after dinner, stop at your first meal: right from your Lock Screen or widget, without opening the app.
  • Choose 12, 14, 16, 18, 20 or 24 hours, or your own length up to 7 days (also for Lent).
  • Forgot to stop? Adjust start and end afterwards.
  • No streaks, no badges, no missed days. If you start again, Stilvast simply says: welcome back.
  • Notifications are off, except "goal reached". Export everything as CSV whenever you like.

What it costs

Free to download. Timing always stays free. Everything open for 14 days; then a single purchase of € 4.99 (launch price until 1 April 2027, then € 6.99; if you bought it, you keep everything) for the Live Activity and your history; export stays free. Family Sharing included. Nothing is ever charged automatically.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no trackers. Everything stays on your phone; the app makes no network requests of its own. The App Store shows the label "Data Not Collected". Privacy policy ›

Good to know

Stilvast is a timer, not a diet and not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before fasting if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, have or have had an eating disorder, have diabetes or take medication.

Help and contact

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Open Stilvast, go to More and tap Restore purchase, using the same Apple ID.

My family wants to join in.

With Apple Family Sharing one purchase covers your whole family. Turn it on in your iPhone settings under Family.

How do I take my fasts to another app?

Go to More and choose Export as CSV. You can save or share the file.

What happens to my data if I delete the app?

It's gone, unless you use iCloud backup or export first. We have no copy.

Question not listed? Email contact@peulen.app. We read everything ourselves.
