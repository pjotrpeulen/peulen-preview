Privacy policy

Our apps collect no data. This website uses no cookies and no tracking.

Who we are

Peulen is the brand of the brothers Pjotr and Kasper Peulen. Contact: contact@peulen.app. We will list our company details (such as the Chamber of Commerce number) here once our apps are in the App Store.

What our apps collect

Nothing. What you enter (for example your fasting times) stays on your phone, and, in an app that lets you share, also in your own iCloud. Our apps contain no third-party analytics, advertising or crash services, which is why the App Store shows "Data Not Collected".

Backup and sharing

If you use iCloud backup, your phone stores your data encrypted in your own iCloud. If you share your list with your partner in Eerste hapjes, that also goes through iCloud. That is between you and Apple; we can't access it.

Payments

Purchases go through Apple; the App Store lists Kasper Peulen as the seller. We receive no name, email or payment details; Apple only reports anonymous sales figures.

If you email us

We only use your address and message to reply, keep it no longer than needed to handle your question, and share it with no one.

This website

No cookies, no tracking, no external fonts or scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.); GitHub records technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site securely. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

You can always ask what data we have about you (usually none), and have it corrected or deleted. You can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Updated

18 November 2026: Your Cheerleader, Eerste hapjes, Way & Virtue, Adem en Stil and Tartil added.

Per app

Stilvast

Your fasts (start, end, goal) stay only on your phone, in the app's storage. Notifications are scheduled on your phone. A CSV export is made and shared by you. The app makes no network requests.

Durora

Your city, chosen times and log stay only on your phone. You pick your city without location permission; if you do use your location, it is only used on your phone to calculate times and is not stored or sent. The app makes no network requests.

Your Cheerleader

What you type is only used on your phone to pick a pep talk; it isn't stored or sent. The app only keeps the start of your trial, your purchase and the date of your last free pep talk, on your phone. No iCloud, no export. The app makes no network requests.

Eerste hapjes

Your baby's name and date of birth, your answer about eczema or allergy, the tasting list, the peanut and egg schedule and your notes stay on your phone. If you share the list with your partner, all of this goes via iCloud to the iCloud of you both (Apple, CloudKit); we can't access it and don't see who you share with. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. The PDF overview and CSV export are made and shared by you. The only network traffic is iCloud for sharing.

Way & Virtue (Tao Te Ching)

Your reading place, bookmarks and settings stay only on your phone. A notification is scheduled by your phone (off by default). If you report a mistake in the text, your own mail app opens; the app sends nothing itself. The app makes no network requests.

Adem en Stil

Your sessions (length, your taps when you notice your mind wandering, your pace) stay only on your phone and are included in your normal backup. No iCloud sync of its own, no notifications. The app makes no network requests.

Tartil (Quran)

Your reading place, bookmarks, reading plan and settings stay only on your phone. If you report a mistake in the text, your own mail app opens; the app sends nothing itself. The app makes no network requests and works offline.
