Privacy policy

Our apps collect no data. This website uses no cookies and no tracking.

Who we are

Peulen is the brand of the brothers Pjotr and Kasper Peulen. Contact: contact@peulen.app. We will list our company details (such as the Chamber of Commerce number) here once our apps are in the App Store.

What our apps collect

Nothing. What you enter (for example your fasting times) stays on your phone. Our apps contain no third-party analytics, advertising or crash services, which is why the App Store shows "Data Not Collected".

Backup

If you use iCloud backup, your phone stores your data encrypted in your own iCloud. That is between you and Apple; we can't access it.

Payments

Purchases go through Apple; the App Store lists Kasper Peulen as the seller. We receive no name, email or payment details; Apple only reports anonymous sales figures.

If you email us

We only use your address and message to reply, keep it no longer than needed to handle your question, and share it with no one.

This website

No cookies, no tracking, no external fonts or scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.); GitHub records technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site securely. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

You can always ask what data we have about you (usually none), and have it corrected or deleted. You can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Per app

Stilvast

Your fasts (start, end, goal) stay only on your phone, in the app's storage. Notifications are scheduled on your phone. A CSV export is made and shared by you. The app makes no network requests.

Durora

Your city, chosen times and log stay only on your phone. You pick your city without location permission; if you do use your location, it is only used on your phone to calculate times and is not stored or sent. The app makes no network requests.
