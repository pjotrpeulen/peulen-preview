Your Cheerleader: a pep talk for your big moment

Tell it what you're about to do and get a comically over-the-top pep talk. No questionnaire, no notifications, no account.

Coming soon to the App Store
Not in the App Store yet.

What it does

  • Type what you're about to do, like presentation, exam or job interview, and get a pep talk back, line by line, in big letters.
  • Every line has its own buzz: a tap, a drumroll, a thud.
  • No AI: every line is written by hand.
  • Light and dark mode, English and Dutch.

What it costs

7 days free, then one free pep talk a day, or unlimited with one single purchase. No subscription. You see the price on day one; the trial never quietly turns into a subscription and nothing is ever charged automatically. What you buy stays yours.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no trackers. What you type never leaves your phone and isn't stored; the app makes no network requests of its own. The App Store shows the label "Data Not Collected". Privacy policy ›

Good to know

Type something heavy and Your Cheerleader won't joke: you get a calm reply and the helpline for your country, such as Samaritans in the UK and Ireland (116 123) or 988 in the US, and the emergency number if your life is in danger. Under every pep talk there is always a line with that helpline, also without a purchase. Your Cheerleader is a pep talk with a wink, not a support service.

Terms and seller

Use of the app is covered by Apple’s standard terms for apps (the Licensed Application End User License Agreement). Purchases and refunds go through Apple. The App Store lists the maker as the seller: Kasper Peulen.

Help and contact

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Choose Restore purchase in the app, using the same Apple ID.

Why didn't I get a joke?

With heavy words the app deliberately doesn't joke and shows the helpline. If that happened with something light, email us the word and we'll look into it.

What happens to what I type?

Nothing. It stays on your phone, isn't stored and goes nowhere.

Question not listed? Email contact@peulen.app. We read everything ourselves.
